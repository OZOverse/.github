# OZOverse

**A universe to explore, create, and grow.**

OZOverse is an AI-native platform for personalized human growth, beginning with children. Our first world, First Garden, is in development for ages 5–7, with an adult alongside.

[Website](https://ozoverse.ai) · [Vision](https://ozoverse.ai/vision.html) · [Safety & Privacy](https://ozoverse.ai/safety.html)

## What we are building

- **First Garden** — a living world that responds to what a child does in it.
- **BlueStar** — the first AI companion within the OZOverse Platform. Warmth, yes. Dependency, never.
- **Living Atlas** — a record of each Explorer's own journey, always viewable and erasable by the family.
- **The OZOverse Platform** — the layer that connects worlds, companions, and AI capabilities around the journey. The intelligence can change. The human journey remains.

## Our commitments

- Agency over engagement. We do not optimize for screen time.
- Human relationships over artificial attachment.
- Privacy and dignity by design. We do not sell children's data or advertise to children.
- Safety before scale.
